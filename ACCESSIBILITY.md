# Accessibility

Rotting Research helps researchers check links cited in their work. We want
people with disabilities to be able to use the service, read project
documentation, and participate in the project.

## Accessibility goals

We aim to make the service and project materials usable with a range of access
needs and assistive technologies. When building or changing the project, we
intend to consider keyboard access, clear labels and instructions, meaningful
structure, readable text and contrast, and useful text alternatives for
informative images.

## Supported environments and known barriers

The service is web-based. We do not currently publish a tested browser and
assistive-technology support matrix, an accessibility audit, or a conformance
claim against a particular standard. We also do not maintain a complete,
verified list of accessibility barriers. This means we cannot guarantee that
every part of the service is accessible, and the absence of a listed barrier
does not mean there are none.

## Report an accessibility barrier

Please [open an issue in the project repository](https://github.com/rottingresearch/rottingresearch/issues)
to report a barrier or suggest an improvement. If possible, include:

- The page or feature where you encountered the barrier.
- The steps you took and what happened, including what you expected to happen.
- Your browser and operating system, and any assistive technology or relevant
  settings you were using.

Please do not include private research documents, personal information, or
other confidential material in a public issue. You can describe the problem
without attaching the document you were working with.

## Contributors and maintenance

We welcome accessibility improvements and reports from people with disabilities
and assistive-technology users. Contributors changing the interface should
consider keyboard operation, semantic structure, accessible names and
instructions, and text alternatives as part of the change.

Project maintainers review accessibility reports through the repository's
issue tracker and consider them alongside other project work. There is no
published response-time commitment for accessibility reports.
